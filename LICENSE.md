## License Selection Guide

### Choose AGPL-3.0 if:
- You are using the software for personal or internal open-source projects
- You are okay with open-sourcing your modifications
- You are deploying in a single-tenant environment
- You don't need multi-tenant or white-label features
- You are comfortable with the network copyleft requirements

### Choose Commercial License if:
- You need multi-tenant or white-label capabilities
- You want to keep your modifications proprietary
